OUR JOURNEY

From a college to a university.

Kristu Jayanti College was founded in 1999 and is run by Bodhi Niketan Trust, formed by members of the St. Joseph Province of the Carmelites of Mary Immaculate (CMI).

1999

Kristu Jayanti College was founded.

Growth

Today
